# Marketing Policy

**Document owner:** Marketing & Channels Team
**Applies to:** All customer-facing campaigns, channels, and messaging
**Last reviewed:** Q3 FY26

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines which acquisition and retention channels are approved,
how budget should be allocated across them, and what tone/content rules
apply to any campaign copy — including copy drafted or suggested by AI
tools.

## 2. Approved Channels

- **Organic (SEO/content):** always approved, no spend cap.
- **Paid Ads (search + social):** approved; budget changes above 20% of
  current monthly spend require Marketing Lead sign-off.
- **Email:** approved for existing customers only; must respect
  unsubscribe status and send-frequency caps (see Section 5).
- **Social Media (organic + influencer):** approved; influencer
  partnerships above a fixed cost threshold require legal review for
  disclosure compliance.
- **Referral:** approved and actively encouraged — referral customers
  consistently show strong order value, so referral-incentive budget may
  be scaled ahead of other channels without additional approval, up to a
  20% quarter-over-quarter increase.

## 3. Channel Investment Guidance

- Channels demonstrating an **Average Order Value (AOV) at least 10% above
  the platform-wide average** are candidates for increased investment,
  provided the increase is funded from reallocated (not new) budget unless
  pre-approved.
- Channels with rising customer acquisition cost (CAC) but flat or falling
  AOV should be flagged for review before further scaling, not
  automatically cut — a short diagnostic period is expected first.
- Paid channels should not be scaled purely because a short-term spike in
  performance was observed; a minimum of 2 consecutive months of the
  signal is required before recommending a budget increase.

## 4. Messaging & Tone Guidelines

- All copy must be factually accurate — no fabricated scarcity ("only 2
  left!") unless inventory data genuinely supports the claim.
- Tone should be confident and helpful, not high-pressure. Avoid manufactured
  urgency language for non-time-limited offers.
- Do not make comparative claims against named competitors without a
  substantiated, cited basis.
- All discount or promotional messaging must comply with the Promotion
  Guidelines document — marketing copy may not imply a larger discount than
  what Pricing has approved.

## 5. Customer Communication Limits

- Maximum 3 marketing emails per customer per week, regardless of channel
  overlap.
- Customers who have not engaged with (opened) the last 5 consecutive
  emails should be moved to a reduced-frequency or win-back-specific track
  rather than continuing standard send cadence.
- Any communication targeting customers identified as churn-risk should
  lead with value (loyalty recognition, personalized recommendations)
  before leading with a discount.

## 6. Segment-Based Campaign Guidance

- **New customers:** welcome series focused on product education and
  trust-building (reviews, guarantees), not immediate discounting.
- **High-value / loyal customers:** recognition-led campaigns (early
  access, exclusive previews) are preferred over generic discount blasts.
- **Churn-risk customers:** win-back campaigns may combine a modest,
  policy-compliant discount (see Pricing Policy Section 4) with a direct,
  personal message referencing their past purchase history.

## 7. Compliance

- All campaigns must comply with applicable consumer-protection and
  data-privacy regulations for the customer's region.
- Any campaign using customer purchase history for personalization must
  do so in aggregate/segment form unless the customer has explicitly
  opted in to individualized marketing.
