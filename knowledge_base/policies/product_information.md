# Product Information & Catalog Guidelines

**Document owner:** Catalog & Merchandising Team
**Applies to:** All product listings across the catalog
**Last reviewed:** Q3 FY26

## 1. Catalog Structure

The catalog is organized into six top-level categories, each with defined
sub-categories:

- **Electronics** — Audio, Wearables, Accessories, Smart Home
- **Apparel** — Menswear, Womenswear, Footwear, Winterwear
- **Home & Kitchen** — Cookware, Storage, Decor, Appliances
- **Beauty & Personal Care** — Skincare, Haircare, Fragrance
- **Sports & Outdoors** — Fitness, Outdoor Gear, Team Sports
- **Books & Stationery** — Fiction, Non-Fiction, Office Supplies

Every SKU must belong to exactly one category and one sub-category. New
sub-categories require Merchandising Lead approval before products are
listed under them.

## 2. Product Data Requirements

Every listed product must have:
- A unique product ID
- A clear, non-duplicated product name
- Accurate cost price and selling price (used for margin calculations —
  see Pricing Policy)
- A launch date, used for lifecycle and "new arrival" tagging
- At least one high-resolution image and a factual description (no
  unverifiable superlative claims like "the best on the market")

## 3. Lifecycle Stages

- **New Arrival:** first 30 days after launch date; eligible for
  "New Arrival" placement but not for clearance-level discounting.
- **Core / Steady Seller:** established products with consistent order
  history; standard pricing and promotion rules apply.
- **Slow-Moving:** products with low unit sales relative to their
  category and time-on-catalog. These are candidates for either a
  bundling promotion (if margin allows, per Promotion Guidelines) or a
  catalog review for potential delisting if consistently unsold after 6+
  months.
- **Clearance:** explicitly tagged by Merchandising for end-of-life
  inventory; eligible for the discount depths defined in the Pricing
  Policy's clearance carve-out.

## 4. Margin Bands (for reference)

- **Thin margin:** below 20% — handle with care per Pricing Policy
  Section 2; avoid additional discounting.
- **Standard margin:** 20–50% — normal pricing and promotion rules apply.
- **High margin:** above 50% — good candidates for promotional pushes,
  bundling, or marketing spend, since there's room to invest in moving
  volume without breaching the margin floor.

## 5. Category Notes Relevant to Growth Planning

- **Apparel** shows the strongest seasonal concentration in the catalog,
  with demand typically peaking around festive/holiday periods. Inventory
  and marketing planning for this category should anticipate that window
  rather than react to it.
- **Electronics** and **Home & Kitchen** carry higher average price points
  but more price sensitivity; bundling tends to outperform straight
  discounting here.
- **Books & Stationery** has the lowest average margin in the catalog and
  should not be used as a discount-driven growth lever.
- **Beauty & Personal Care** and **Sports & Outdoors** are well suited to
  repeat-purchase and loyalty-based retention plays rather than one-time
  discount campaigns.

## 6. Returns & Quality

- Standard return window is 15 days from delivery for all categories
  except Beauty & Personal Care (7 days, hygiene-sensitive) and
  Electronics (30 days, manufacturer-defect coverage).
- Products with a return rate meaningfully above their category average
  should be flagged for a quality review before being featured in any
  promotional campaign.

## 7. Restricted Claims

Product listings and any AI-generated product copy may not claim medical,
therapeutic, or health outcomes for non-regulated products (applies
especially to Beauty & Personal Care and Sports & Outdoors items) unless
the product carries the relevant regulatory certification on file.
