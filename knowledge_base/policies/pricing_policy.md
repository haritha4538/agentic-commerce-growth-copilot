# Pricing Policy

**Document owner:** Revenue & Pricing Team
**Applies to:** All product categories sold through the platform
**Last reviewed:** Q3 FY26

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how prices, discounts, and margin floors are set and
protected across the catalog. Any AI-generated or human-authored pricing
recommendation must comply with the rules below before it is actioned.

## 2. Margin Floor Rules

- No product may be sold, after all discounts and promotional stacking, at
  a realized margin below **15%** of selling price, except for approved
  clearance SKUs explicitly tagged `clearance` in the product catalog.
- Products with a listed margin below 20% at full price ("thin-margin
  SKUs") may not be discounted by more than **5%** under any circumstance
  without written sign-off from the Pricing Lead.
- Products with a margin above 50% ("high-margin SKUs") may be discounted
  up to **25%** as part of a growth or awareness campaign, provided the
  realized margin after discount does not fall below the 15% floor.

## 3. Discount Authorization Tiers

| Discount Depth | Who Can Approve                     |
|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| 0–10%          | Category Manager                     |
| 11–20%         | Category Manager + Pricing Lead      |
| 21–35%         | Head of Growth (case-by-case)        |
| 35%+           | Not permitted outside clearance events |

## 4. Customer-Segment Pricing

- Discounts targeted at **high-value or loyal customers** (e.g., top RFM
  segments) should prioritize non-price levers first: early access, free
  shipping, loyalty points, or bundled gifts. Price discounting to
  already-loyal, high-monetary customers erodes margin without meaningfully
  changing their behavior and should be a last resort.
- Discounts aimed at **win-back / churn-risk customers** (no purchase in
  60+ days) may go up to the category's normal discount ceiling, since the
  goal is reactivation, not margin optimization, for this segment.
- **New customer** discounts (first purchase) are capped at 15% platform-wide
  to avoid training new customers to wait for markdowns.

## 5. Category-Specific Notes

- **Electronics:** price-sensitive category; discounting is effective but
  margins are already thin — prioritize bundling over direct price cuts.
- **Apparel:** highly seasonal; end-of-season markdowns up to 40% are
  pre-approved during the designated clearance window (see Promotion
  Guidelines) but not outside it.
- **Beauty & Personal Care:** repeat-purchase category; favor
  subscribe-and-save or sample-led discovery over deep discounts.
- **Books & Stationery:** lowest average margin category; avoid additional
  discounting beyond standard tiers above.

## 6. Prohibited Pricing Practices

- No price may be raised immediately before a "discount" is applied to
  create an inflated reference price (deceptive pricing).
- Prices must not vary by customer in a way that could be perceived as
  discriminatory (e.g., by demographic group). Personalization is limited
  to segment-based offers described in Section 4.
- Any price change affecting more than 50 SKUs at once requires Pricing
  Lead review before going live.

## 7. Review Cadence

This policy is reviewed quarterly. Recommendations generated by any
internal tool (including AI-assisted strategy tools) must cite this
document's current version and may not override the margin floor or
authorization tiers above.
