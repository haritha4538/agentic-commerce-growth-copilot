# Promotion Guidelines

**Document owner:** Growth & Commercial Operations
**Applies to:** All time-bound promotions, sales events, and bundles
**Last reviewed:** Q3 FY26

## 1. Purpose

Defines which promotion types are permitted, how they may be combined
("stacked"), and the minimum conditions required before a promotion goes
live. These guidelines work together with the Pricing Policy (margin
floors) and Marketing Policy (channel/messaging rules).

## 2. Approved Promotion Types

- **Percentage-off discounts** — must respect the discount authorization
  tiers in the Pricing Policy.
- **Bundle offers** (e.g., "buy 2 get 1 at reduced price") — preferred over
  straight discounts for high-margin, low-volume products, since bundling
  moves inventory without permanently anchoring the customer's price
  expectation downward.
- **Free shipping thresholds** — always approved as a lever; does not
  require Pricing sign-off since it does not alter product price.
- **Loyalty point multipliers** — approved for high-value/loyal customer
  campaigns as an alternative to direct discounting.
- **Seasonal clearance events** — approved twice yearly (end-of-season
  windows) with pre-cleared discount depths up to 40% on Apparel and up to
  30% on other categories.

## 3. Seasonal & Category Timing

- Categories that show a historical revenue spike in a given month (for
  example, a category peaking during a festive or holiday period) should
  have inventory and promotional creative ready **at least 3 weeks before**
  the expected peak, not during or after it.
- Promotions launched to "chase" a spike already in progress are
  discouraged — the priority for an in-progress spike is inventory
  availability and fulfillment speed, not additional discounting, since
  demand is already elevated.
- Outside of identified seasonal windows, categories should not be given
  clearance-level discount depths; use standard promotion types instead.

## 4. Stacking Rules

- A maximum of **one** percentage-off promotion and **one** non-price lever
  (free shipping, loyalty multiplier, or bundle) may be combined per order.
- Two percentage-off promotions may never be stacked.
- Referral incentives may be combined with any other active promotion,
  since referral-driven orders have historically shown healthy order
  value and are treated as an acquisition cost, not a margin giveaway.

## 5. Minimum Conditions Before Launch

A promotion may only go live once all of the following are confirmed:
1. The discount depth complies with the Pricing Policy's authorization
   tier and margin floor.
2. Sufficient inventory exists to fulfill realistic expected demand
   (avoid promoting products at high risk of stock-out or, conversely,
   already slow-moving dead stock without a bundling rationale).
3. The messaging has been checked against the Marketing Policy's tone and
   claims rules.
4. A defined start and end date is set — open-ended promotions are not
   permitted, as they erode the effectiveness of future time-bound offers.

## 6. Suitable Candidates for Promotion

Products and segments that make good promotion candidates:
- **High-margin, low-volume products**: strong bundling or awareness-push
  candidates, since the margin cushion supports a discount without
  breaching the pricing floor.
- **Categories approaching a known seasonal spike**: benefit from early
  promotional and inventory readiness rather than reactive discounting.
- **Churn-risk, previously high-value customers**: personalized win-back
  offers combining a modest discount with loyalty recognition.

Products and segments that are **poor** promotion candidates:
- Thin-margin SKUs already near their margin floor.
- Products currently experiencing a demand spike (they don't need a
  discount to sell; the priority is stock availability).
- Broad, untargeted discounting to already-loyal high-value customers,
  which trains habitual discount-seeking behavior without incremental
  benefit.

## 7. Review

All promotions above 25% discount depth or running longer than 14 days
require Growth Lead sign-off before launch, in addition to standard
Pricing Policy approval.
